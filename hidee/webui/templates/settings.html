Your data

  • Nothing is sent to us. No account, no telemetry. Hidee checks for updates only if you said yes, and only for the version number. Otherwise it only passes on what your agent was already sending, to where it was already going.
  • It stays on this computer. Everything Hidee keeps is in one folder that only your user account can open.
  • It keeps as little as it can. Long text is kept as a short excerpt with secrets masked, plus a fingerprint of the whole.
  • You can see all of it. The files are plain text and SQLite; you can read, copy, or delete them.
