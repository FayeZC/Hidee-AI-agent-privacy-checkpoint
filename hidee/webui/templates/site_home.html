01 What Hidee watches for

Four kinds of risk, named in plain words.

Privacy

Names, emails, phone numbers, and sensitive facts about real people, even written as plain sentences.

maria.lopez@example.org

Security

API keys, card numbers, credential files, and commands that ask for more power.

~/.ssh/id_ed25519

Internal

Confidential files, private network addresses, and local paths that should stay inside.

q3-plan.pdf · CONFIDENTIAL

Overreach

Steps outside the folder you gave the agent, or into places it has no reason to go.

~/Documents/budget.xlsx

02 How it works

Ordinary work goes through. Sensitive steps wait for you.

{% with p="story" %}{% include "_flow_art.html" %}{% endwith %}
  1. 1

    Start a session

    Hidee opens your AI agent in its own terminal, with a checkpoint in front of it. Your own agent settings stay as they are.

  2. 2

    Watch the flow

    Every tool call moves across a live map, from the session through Hidee to where it goes. Ordinary steps pass without a question.

  3. 3

    Decide what leaves

    When a step would carry something sensitive, or a message would go out in your name, it waits. Send a shorter version, approve it, save it as a draft, or hold it back.

03 Know deeper

Click any step to see exactly what it carried.

Hidee can show you your data flow at a glance. No technical background? No need to worry: each step opens as a simple picture of what the agent wanted to send, what Hidee let through, and what it kept back.

04 Two layers

It reads the step, and it watches the wire.

The hook

Before each tool call runs, Hidee reads what it says. When a command runs a script, Hidee reads the script too.

The network gate

Every connection the session makes passes through Hidee, including scripts, package managers, and the tools the agent starts. You see where each one goes.

05 Your data

Your data stays on your computer.

  • Runs on your computer. The checkpoint, the map, and every record live in ~/.hidee.
  • No account. Install it, start it, and use it. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Nothing sent to us. Hidee has no telemetry. It only talks to the places your agent was already going.
